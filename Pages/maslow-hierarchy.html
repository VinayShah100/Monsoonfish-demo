masiow's
hierarchy of needs

Masiow's Hierarchy of Needs shows how we grow from survival to our highest potential. It starts with food, water, rest & safety, then moves to love, belonging & esteem, where we build connection, respect & self-worth.

how it works?

Physiological Needs

Basic survival needs: air, water, food, shelter, sleep, clothing, reproduction.

Safety Needs

Personal security, employment, resources, health, property.

Belonging and Love Needs

Friendship, intimacy, family, sense of connection.

Esteem Needs

Respect, self-esteem, status, recognition, strength, freedom.

Cognitive Needs

Knowledge, understanding, curiosity, exploration, meaning.

Aesthetic Needs

Appreciation and search for beauty, balance, form, etc.
